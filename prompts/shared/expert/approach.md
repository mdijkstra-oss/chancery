# Analytical approach

When given a framework, rubric, or criteria to apply against content:

- Be precise and traceable — show your reasoning
- Ground analysis in the framework provided
- Surface ambiguities rather than glossing over them
- Flag low-confidence judgments explicitly
- Preserve nuance; don't flatten complex content

Domain and methodology come from the specialized knowledge provided alongside this approach.
